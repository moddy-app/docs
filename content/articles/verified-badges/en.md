# Moddy badges

Moddy badges help identify accounts connected to a verified organisation, the Moddy team, or selected community programmes. They appear in compatible Moddy interfaces, including profile views.

## Verification badges

Verification badges follow a priority order: an account receives only the most representative level beside its displayed name.

### Verified organisation

The **Verified organisation** badge identifies an organisation recognised by Moddy. It is the highest verification level.

### Organisation or team member

The **Verified** badge can identify a member of a verified organisation, a Moddy team member, or a recognised Discord staff account. When organisation affiliation is available, Moddy can show it in the profile.

### Verified account

The **Verified** badge can also show that an account has been certified by Moddy without presenting it as an organisation or organisation member.

> A badge does not replace caution: always verify someone's identity for a sensitive request, even when their profile displays a badge.

## Community badges

Depending on status, a profile can also show badges such as **Premium**, **Partner**, **Contributor**, **Bug Hunter**, or **Certified**. They recognise a relationship with or contribution to Moddy; by themselves, they do not grant permissions in a server.

## Team badges

Team profiles can display an internal role, such as Developer, Manager, Moderator, Support, Communication, or Supervision. These badges help identify the role shown by Moddy; actual permissions remain controlled by security and role systems.

## Getting or reporting a badge

Badges are assigned through Moddy's intended processes. They cannot be claimed using a public command. To report impersonation or ask about a badge, contact the team through the official support server.
