# Moderation and security

Moddy brings moderation actions and their history together in a case system. It improves follow-up without replacing your team's judgement.

## Moderation actions

Moderation commands help address behaviour that breaks your rules. Discord permissions and the permissions defined for your team decide who can use them.

## Cases and history

`/cases` opens the server case browser for authorised staff. `/mycases` lets a user review their own cases in relevant servers. A case can include a reason, evidence, sanctions, and related comments.

## Appeals

Sanctions created by supported flows can offer an appeal. Requests are kept separate from moderation decisions and reviewed by authorised people.

## Global sanctions

Moddy global sanctions are reserved for the Moddy team. They are not an ordinary moderation tool for server administrators.

## Reduce mistakes

Record the reason, verify the target's identity before confirming, and use proportionate sanctions. Use cases to keep a reliable record of decisions.
