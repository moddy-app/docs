# AI AutoMod

The **AI AutoMod** module helps detect content that conflicts with your community's configured rules. It combines deterministic checks with contextual analysis so that every message is not treated as the same kind of case.

## Before enabling it

Write clear rules and define content categories. Configure a notification channel so your team can review decisions and adjust settings.

## How it works

The module filters clearly safe messages, identifies known signals, and can analyse more ambiguous cases. History and severity influence the suggested response. Important decisions are designed to remain reviewable.

## Useful settings

- Start with the most conservative actions.
- Set a sanction ceiling that matches your rules.
- Use review or simulation modes when available.
- Review the log and created cases regularly.

## Limits

An automated decision is never absolute truth. Your team must be able to correct an action, account for context, and handle appeals fairly.
