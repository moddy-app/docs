# Everyday commands

Moddy's global commands can be used in servers, DMs with the bot, and—when supported—through personal installations.

## Look up Discord information

- `/user` shows public information about a user or bot.
- `/avatar` and `/banner` open available profile media.
- `/invite` inspects a Discord invite.
- `/emoji` provides emoji information and related actions.

## Manage your time and preferences

- `/preferences` manages settings including default privacy.
- `/reminder` creates and manages personal reminders.
- `/saved-messages` helps you retrieve saved messages.
- `/roll` runs simple random draws for decisions or activities.
- `/subscription` shows subscription information associated with your account.

## Messages and webhooks

`/webhook` lets you inspect and manage webhooks you can access. Never post a webhook URL publicly: it can be used to send messages to its channel.

## Tip

Use **incognito** for a personal lookup or a response you do not want to show in the channel.
